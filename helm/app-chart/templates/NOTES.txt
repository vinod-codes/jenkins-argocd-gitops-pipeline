The gitops-demo application is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Check the pods:
  kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Check the Service:
  kubectl get svc -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Port-forward to try the app locally:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "app-chart.fullname" . }} 8080:80 -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
  curl http://localhost:8080/health
